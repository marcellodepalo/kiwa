Privacy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how the Kiwa app for iPhone and iPhone Duo (“Kiwa”, “the app”) and this website handle your personal data. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Italian Personal Data Protection Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003 as amended), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and Apple’s App Store requirements.

In short

  • Kiwa has no account and no sign-up.
  • Everything you create in Kiwa — actions, expenses, notes, people, tags and settings — stays on your device. It is not sent to me or to any server I run.
  • Kiwa contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • I do not sell or share your personal data, and I do not track you across apps or websites.
  • Kiwa is currently free. There are no in-app purchases and no payment data is handled.

Who is responsible

The data controller for this website and for any message you send me is Marcello De Palo, the developer of Kiwa. You can reach me at marcellodepalo@gmail.com.

For the data that stays on your device, nothing is transmitted to me, so I do not process it: you remain in control of it at all times.

Data stored on your device

The actions, expenses, notes, people, tags, time zones and preferences you create are saved on your iPhone, in the app and in a storage area it shares only with the Kiwa widget so the widget can show your day. This data is protected by iOS and is included in your device backups according to your own iCloud or computer backup settings. Deleting the app deletes this data from the device.

Permissions and what they are used for

Kiwa asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. Every permission can be refused or revoked at any time in the iOS Settings app; the rest of Kiwa keeps working without it.

  • Calendars and Reminders — to show, create and edit your events and to keep your actions in step with your calendars. Kiwa reads and writes them through iOS on your device. If your calendars are synced with a service such as iCloud, Google or Exchange, that sync is performed by iOS and governed by that provider’s privacy policy.
  • Contacts — to let you mention people in events, actions and expenses. Contacts are read on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Location — to show the weather for where you are. See “Apple services” below.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — to dictate notes on your events and actions. See “Apple services” below.
  • Camera and Photos — to attach photos to events and to read an expense from a receipt you photograph. Only the photos you choose are used, and they stay on your device.
  • Siri — to let you ask for your actions and events by voice.

Apple services

Some features rely on services provided by Apple. When you use them, the data needed for the request is sent to Apple, not to me, and is handled under Apple’s Privacy Policy:

  • Weather (Apple Weather / WeatherKit) — your location, or the place you choose, is sent to Apple to fetch the forecast. Apple states that this location is not associated with your Apple Account.
  • Place search (Apple Maps) — the text you type when looking up a place for an event or for the weather is sent to Apple to find matching places.
  • Dictation (Speech Recognition) — when your device supports it, speech is recognised entirely on the device. If on-device recognition is not available, the audio is sent to Apple to be transcribed.
  • Siri — voice requests are processed by Apple as described in Apple’s Siri privacy information.

Data I collect

The app does not collect any personal data. In App Store terms: Data Not Collected.

If you share crash reports or usage statistics with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may make anonymised crash logs and aggregate statistics available to me through App Store Connect. These do not identify you and are used only to fix problems.

If you write to me by email, I use your address and your message only to answer you, on the basis of my legitimate interest in replying to your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I keep the correspondence only as long as needed to handle it, and in any case no longer than 24 months.

This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, records technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the service secure; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The page font is loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google servers and shares your IP address with them; see the Google Privacy Policy. These transfers may involve the United States and rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or on the providers’ Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Because the app keeps your data on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly: edit or delete items in Kiwa, revoke permissions in the iOS Settings app, or delete the app. For anything else, write to me and I will reply within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

California residents: I do not sell or share personal information, and I do not use or disclose sensitive personal information. You have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information and will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Children

Kiwa is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children. Since the app collects no personal data, it does not collect data from children. If you believe a child has sent me personal data by email, contact me and I will delete it.

Security

Your data is protected by the security of iOS, including the encryption of your device when it is locked with a passcode. Keeping your device and your backups secure keeps your Kiwa data secure.

Changes to this policy

If Kiwa changes the way it handles data — for example if a paid version or a sync feature is introduced — this page will be updated before the change takes effect, and the date at the top will change. Significant changes will also be announced in the app’s release notes.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, write to marcellodepalo@gmail.com.
